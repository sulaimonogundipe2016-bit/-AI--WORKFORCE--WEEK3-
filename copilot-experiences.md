Copilot Chat
Copilot Chat is an AI-driven prompt-and-response tool designed for general productivity and collaboration. It integrates seamlessly with Microsoft 365 applications like Teams, Outlook, and Edge. Users can interact with it to generate content, summarize documents, create meeting agendas, or answer questions using both internet and enterprise data. It is included with a Microsoft 365 subscription and offers enterprise-grade data protection. This tool is ideal for users who need AI assistance for day-to-day tasks, such as drafting emails, summarizing reports, or managing workflows.Copilot Studio
Copilot Studio is a low-code platform that allows organizations to create and customize AI agents tailored to their specific business needs. These agents can automate and execute business processes, such as help desk operations, change management, or meeting guest management. Users can connect these agents to various data sources and design custom prompts to enhance their functionality. Copilot Studio is licensed by organizations and is best suited for IT professionals or developers looking to build customized AI solutions for enterprise workflows.
